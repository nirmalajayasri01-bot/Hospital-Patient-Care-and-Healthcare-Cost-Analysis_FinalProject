Project Summary
Hospital Patient Care and Healthcare Cost Analysis: Admissions, Length of Stay, Insurance Coverage, and Medical Procedures

This project analyzes synthetic healthcare data from approximately 974 patients and 27,891 medical encounters recorded at Massachusetts General Hospital between 2011 and 2022. The dataset contains information on patient demographics, hospital visits, healthcare costs, insurance coverage, and medical encounters, enabling a comprehensive assessment of hospital operations and patient care patterns.

The primary objective of this study is to understand patient admission trends, evaluate hospital utilization, analyze healthcare costs, and examine the impact of insurance coverage on medical services. The analysis investigates how frequently patients are admitted or readmitted, the average duration of hospital stays, and the financial burden associated with healthcare visits.

Additionally, the project explores the proportion of medical procedures and encounters covered by insurance, helping to identify the role of insurance providers in reducing patient expenses. By examining encounter types such as inpatient, outpatient, emergency, ambulatory, and urgent care visits, the study provides valuable insights into healthcare service utilization and resource allocation.

Dataset Overview
Patients: 974
Medical Encounters: 27,891
Study Period: 2011–2022
Encounter Types: Inpatient, Outpatient, Emergency, Ambulatory, Urgent Care, Wellness
Key Variables: Patient demographics, encounter costs, insurance coverage, encounter classes, admission dates, discharge dates
Analysis Objectives
Analyze patient admissions and readmissions over time.
Calculate the average hospital length of stay.
Evaluate the average cost per medical encounter.
Assess insurance coverage across healthcare services.
Identify trends in healthcare utilization and patient care.
Generate insights to support hospital management and healthcare decision-making.
Key Insights from the Dataset
Ambulatory and outpatient visits account for the majority of encounters.
Inpatient admissions represent a smaller but more resource-intensive portion of healthcare services.
The average claim cost per encounter is approximately $3,640.
Insurance contributes significantly toward healthcare expenses, covering costs in many encounters.
The dataset provides opportunities to study patient flow, healthcare spending, and operational efficiency.
Expected Outcomes
Better understanding of patient admission patterns.
Insights into hospital resource utilization.
Evaluation of healthcare costs and insurance effectiveness.
Identification of trends that can support healthcare planning and policy decisions.
Data-driven recommendations for improving patient care and operational efficiency.

This analysis serves as a practical application of healthcare analytics, demonstrating how patient and encounter data can be transformed into actionable insights for improving hospital performance and healthcare delivery.
